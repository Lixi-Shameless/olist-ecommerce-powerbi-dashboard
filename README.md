🔑 Key Insights & Findings
Peak Sales Period: Highest revenue occurred around Late 2017 to Early 2018 (Black Friday & Q1 Shopping Season).

Top Category: Health & Beauty leads with R$ 1.23M in total revenue.

Regional Performance: São Paulo (SP) dominates regional revenue with R$ 5.1M.

Customer Satisfaction: Over 57K orders received a 5-star rating (~60%+ overall satisfaction).

Payment Preference: Credit Card accounts for the majority of sales volume.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Features
Data Visualization: Power BI Desktop

Data Modeling: Star Schema Architecture (olist_orders, olist_customers, olist_order_items, olist_order_payments, olist_order_reviews)

DAX Calculations: Custom measures for Revenue, Total Orders, AOV, Avg Review Score, and On-Time Delivery Rate.
